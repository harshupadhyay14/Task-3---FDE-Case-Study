**Re:** Fixing tile shade mismatches at batch change: what we propose, what we need, what it costs

Thank you for the detailed call. Here is our proposal in plain terms.

**What we propose**
Today, when a repeat SKU comes out in the wrong shade, your QC team fixes the print file by eye, prints six or seven variants, fires them, and checks them in the lab. Each round takes 6-7 hours, and the line keeps running meanwhile. You already measure the colour gap in the lab. What is missing is a way to turn that gap into "change this colour by this much".

We will build a lab tool that does exactly that. QC measures the master tile and the first production tile. The tool tells them which colours to change in the print file, and by how much. QC approves, prints one confirmation tile, and releases the batch. The recommendation takes about 5 minutes. Firing the tile still takes time, so the realistic goal is **about 2 hours to an approved file, instead of several 6-7 hour rounds**, not 5 minutes end to end.

We would not put cameras on the line, connect to your machines, or look for root causes yet. Those are bigger and riskier, and your own view is that they are harder.

**What we need from you**
- A signed order and NDA, so we can start.
- Sample tiles (the master and the variation tiles you offered), with their print files and lab readings, in week 1.
- A site visit and access to the QC and design team. They are the people who will use this, and we want them on the next call.
- About 4 hours a week from one QC engineer, 2 hours a week from a project owner, and 1-2 days from IT to host a small server inside the plant.
- Your tolerance for "matching", agreed with QC.
- Records of your last 20 or so shade corrections, if they exist.

On your question about OEMs and hardware: we are a software team. We work with the instruments you already have. If a better instrument is ever needed, we will specify it and you would buy it directly. We do not resell hardware.

**What it costs**
A fixed-fee, 16-week pilot on one line at the India plant: **USD 85,000**.
- 30% on signing
- 30% when you accept the first demo on your own tiles (week 4)
- 40% only if the pilot meets the results we agree up front (week 16)

If the pilot works, a yearly licence is **USD 120,000 per plant**, covering all digital lines and support. Based on our assumptions (30 corrections a month, 5,000 m² in the wrong shade each time), the saving could be around USD 1 million a year per plant. We will replace those assumptions with your real numbers in the first two weeks.

**When you will see results**
- **Week 2:** a baseline of today's performance, from your own data.
- **Week 4:** a first demo on your own sample tiles, showing the difference between master and test tile and how much to change.
- **Week 10-11:** first live use in the plant.
- **Week 16:** a pass/fail read-out against the agreed targets.

If the early tests show that the print file is not the main cause of the variation, we will tell you in week 2 and stop before most of the money is spent.
